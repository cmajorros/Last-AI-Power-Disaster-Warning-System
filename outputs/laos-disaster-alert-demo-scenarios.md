# Last AI Power Disaster Warning System Demo Scenarios

Purpose: role-based demo scenes for a 10-minute presentation or screen recording.
Tone: dramatic, inclusive, accessible, and practical.
Live demo: https://laos-alert-mvp-siror-20260612.apac-disaste-1051.chatgpt-team.site/

## Shared Story

It is late afternoon in Khammouane. Heavy rain has continued upstream. The Mekong near Thakhek is rising, the ferry landing may become unsafe, and low riverbank villages could lose road access before night. The platform turns scattered signals into a reviewed alert, sends it through last-mile channels, and tracks whether volunteers carried it into the village.

## Scene 1: Meteorology Officer

User: DMH Vientiane Duty Officer
Goal: Convert forecast risk into an official action.

Steps:

1. Open Dashboard.
2. Switch between English and Lao to show localized data.
3. Click the square Thakhek marker.
4. Choose `Crisis now` or `Disaster now`.
5. Edit the announcement message.
6. Click `Set announcement now`.
7. Confirm the alert and WhatsApp/SMS log appear.

Presenter line:

> The officer turns risk into instruction. The map is no longer decoration; it becomes a decision point.

## Scene 2: AI Intake Reviewer

User: Alert reviewer
Goal: Turn incoming unstructured information into a safe alert draft.

Steps:

1. Open Alerts.
2. Explain supported sources: Gmail, WhatsApp Business, documents, images, screenshots, PDFs, OCR-ready files, and manual uploads.
3. Upload or reference `test.txt`.
4. Add routing notes for elders, children, schools, clinics, rescue boats, pumps, factories, and loudspeakers.
5. Analyze intake.
6. Review evidence, quality flags, target audience, channels, and bilingual messages.
7. Send for review, approve, and publish.

Presenter line:

> AI reduces the drafting delay, but the human reviewer still owns the warning.

## Scene 3: Emergency Response Desk

User: Khammouane PDRRMC Response Desk
Goal: Understand which people, facilities, and resources need action.

Steps:

1. Open Human Map.
2. Select Thakhek riverbank zone.
3. Review affected people and expected start.
4. Point to businesses and factories: rice mill, garment workshop, market, cold storage, ferry landing.
5. Add a response note: `Two boats staged at Thakhek. Request pumps and portable lights from Bolikhamxay Equipment Pool.`

Presenter line:

> Response planning is not only about geography. It is about people, workers, stock, transport, and missing equipment.

## Scene 4: Contact And Equipment Coordinator

User: Logistics or CSO coordinator
Goal: Find who to call when one district lacks equipment.

Steps:

1. Open Contacts.
2. Upload a mock handwritten contact sheet or roster.
3. Run AI detect and record contacts.
4. Ask the contact assistant: `Thakhek does not have enough pumps. Who can we contact to borrow equipment?`
5. Show recommended equipment owner and rescue unit.

Presenter line:

> The database becomes a living phone tree for people, rescue teams, shelters, hospitals, schools, factories, and equipment.

## Scene 5: Village Volunteer

User: Ban Nongbok village volunteer
Goal: Confirm the warning reached households.

Steps:

1. Open Volunteers.
2. Select the published alert.
3. Choose `Village loudspeaker` or `Door-to-door`.
4. Add note: `Elder households closest to the Mekong bank checked first.`
5. Submit acknowledgment.

Presenter line:

> The last mile is human. A log says a message was sent; acknowledgment says someone carried it.

## Scene 6: Community Member Or Subscriber

User: Household, factory worker, parent, or village contact
Goal: Receive warning early enough to prepare.

Steps:

1. Show the final English and Lao message.
2. Explain that future subscriber app users can register location and preferred channel.
3. Explain SMS/WhatsApp alerts for people in affected areas.
4. Show delivery logs and failed-message follow-up.

Presenter line:

> The family should not need to wait for a perfect public announcement if a verified alert can reach their phone and their village volunteer earlier.

## Future Feature Scenes

- AI recommends the best contact persons by disaster area and resource need.
- AI predicts flood probability, water level, and likely start time from live hydromet feeds.
- Subscriber app sends location-based WhatsApp/SMS alerts directly to residents.
- GIS layers show schools, clinics, factories, shelters, ferry points, road closures, and equipment depots.
- Offline volunteer app captures acknowledgment even when connectivity is weak.

## Demo Pass Checklist

- Dashboard loads and language toggle works.
- Square disaster-area markers are clickable.
- Meteorology officer can set an announcement.
- AI intake story covers Gmail, WhatsApp, uploads, OCR, and OpenAI.
- Human approval remains mandatory.
- Human Map shows vulnerable groups and businesses/factories.
- Contacts shows upload and assistant search.
- Volunteers show acknowledgment.
- Reports show logs and failed/pending follow-up.
- Presenter states hosted demo provider sending is simulated.
